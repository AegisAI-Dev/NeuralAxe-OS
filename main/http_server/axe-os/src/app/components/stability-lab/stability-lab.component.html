Stability Lab

Benchmark your own tuning profiles with measured evidence. The Lab applies profiles you choose, collects real telemetry, stops safely when your limits are crossed, and restores your original configuration. It never overclocks on its own, never raises voltage on its own, and never promotes a profile without your explicit action.
